# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome to use and contribute to Clavimo, regardless of experience, background or identity. We want discussions, issues and reviews to be friendly, patient and focused on making the software better.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and help newcomers find their way.
- Respect privacy: never ask others to share passwords, private text or key logs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may block contributors who repeatedly or seriously violate it.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
